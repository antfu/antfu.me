> This is a living post. New redistributions will be added periodically.

In 2024, I started the [Initiative on Sponsorship Forwarding](/posts/sponsorship-forwarding) to help funding flow beyond the most visible projects and maintainers. The idea is simple: you sponsor my work and the tools you benefit from, and I redistribute the funds to the dependencies, infrastructure, and contributors that make that work possible.

The fund is on [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/), and all redistributions are completely transparent. You can see the [full history of sponsorships and redistributions](https://opencollective.com/antfu/transactions) on the [Anthony Fu Fund page](https://opencollective.com/antfu).

I'd like to say thanks for all [the sponsors and contributors](https://opencollective.com/antfu#category-CONTRIBUTE) and support that make this possible. I hope this initiative helps make open source more sustainable and encourages others to support the dependencies they rely on.
